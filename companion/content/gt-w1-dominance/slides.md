## Slide 1 — Games in normal form

- Week 1 of Game Theory
- Players, strategies and payoffs

## Slide 2 — The ingredients

- Players: who makes decisions
- Strategies: the choices each player can make
- Payoffs: what each player gets for every combination of strategies, written in a payoff matrix

## Slide 3 — Dominant strategies

- A strategy strictly dominates another if it gives a higher payoff whatever the other players do
- A rational player never plays a strictly dominated strategy

## Slide 4 — The Prisoner's Dilemma

- Each prisoner can Cooperate (stay silent) or Defect (confess)
- Defect strictly dominates Cooperate for each player
- Yet both would be better off if both cooperated

## Slide 5 — Iterated elimination

- Remove strictly dominated strategies, then look again: new strategies may now be dominated
- Repeat until nothing more can be removed
- Relies on players knowing that the others are rational too
