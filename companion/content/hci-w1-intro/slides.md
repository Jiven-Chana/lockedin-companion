## Slide 1 — What is interaction design?

- Week 1 of Human-Computer Interaction
- Designing interactive products that support the way people communicate and interact

## Slide 2 — Good and poor design

- Poor design makes people adapt to the technology (a remote with forty identical buttons)
- Good design fits what people want to do and the context they do it in

## Slide 3 — Usability goals

- Effectiveness: does it do what it should?
- Efficiency: how much effort does a task take?
- Safety, utility, learnability (easy to learn) and memorability (easy to remember how to use)

## Slide 4 — User experience goals

- How the interaction feels: satisfying, enjoyable, helpful, motivating
- Or undesirable: frustrating, boring, patronising
- User experience goals are subjective; usability goals are more measurable

## Slide 5 — The interaction design lifecycle

- Four activities: discover requirements, design alternatives, prototype, evaluate
- Iterate: evaluation feeds back into requirements and design

## Slide 6 — User-centred design

- Early focus on users and their tasks
- Empirical measurement of how people actually use designs
- Iterative design: repeat the cycle until it works for people
