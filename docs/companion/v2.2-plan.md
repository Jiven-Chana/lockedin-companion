# LockedIn v2.2: what the lo-fi test changed

**Status:** released on 27 Nov 2026 as `lockedin-v2.2`. Approved by Jiven on 27 Nov 2026 ("Approve, but keep the reasoning box open"), with the six decisions in section 2. **Built on:** `lockedin-v2.1`, branch `feat/v2.2`.

**What v2.2 is for:** the lo-fi test (T18, 25 Nov, four testers) reported in `lofi-results.md`, the team's write-up (draft v2, 27 Nov; it is the team's document and is not kept in this repository). v2.2 makes the feature changes its findings point to. How the app looks is v3.0; the desktop app is v4.0 (V-70).

**A caution about the source:** `lofi-results.md` is a draft, and its findings are marked "proposed, to be agreed at T19". v2.2 is built before the group has agreed them. Where v2.2 departs from a proposed change, section 5 says so.

## 4. Choices made for you, to approve with the plan

| Choice | What is built | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Where the on-the-move questions come from | The block's unit's question bank, five questions, sent with the block as v2.1 sends a round. A block whose unit has no bank offers only "Just move the block" | The phone has no AI. The same rule as "Keep going here" (V-46) |
| What an on-the-move answer counts for | The same as any phone answer: it is saved, a wrong one makes a gap, and it cannot close a gap, because the phone has no confidence taps (V-43) | One rule for every phone answer |
| What happens to the place | It changes that block's screen and is written to the event log for the test record. It is not kept in the profile and nothing uses it afterwards | "You tell us. We never track your location." (Q-10) |
| The last on-the-move line | "Anything you got wrong comes back in a later review." The lo-fi says "Anything you missed comes back in Friday's review." | LockedIn never says "missed" (R-6), and a review day is not fixed |
| Where re-planned blocks go | The best free times from today to Sunday, by the planner's own scoring, at most two blocks a day as now. With no room left, the first free times next week | The planner's existing limits (`PLAN_RULES`) |
| "not started" | The label on a passed block. Never "missed", "overdue" or "behind" | R-6 |
| The phone after a re-plan | Shows the moved blocks after the laptop next syncs. The re-plan button is on the laptop only | The planner runs on the laptop |
| Numbers and wording | All in `policy.ts`; the phone's own strings in its `COPY` | Project rule |

## 5. Not in v2.2

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Answer buttons on the lock screen (LF-1) | A web app on an iPhone cannot add buttons to a notification, from what could be found (Apple's forum, 2023, and browser compatibility tables; not confirmed on a 2026 iPhone). Needs a native app |
| An Undo after a phone answer (LF-2) | The proposed change was for lock-screen answers. In the app, the result shows the right answer at once, so an Undo would let a student change their answer after seeing it. The tester who raised it expected fewer mis-taps when the question opens in the app, which is what v2.2 does |
| Focus levels and the shield (LF-5 to LF-7), and a focus timer (LF-12) | Decision 3. A web app cannot see or block other apps |
| A leaderboard or study circle (LF-15) | Decision 5. Asked for by three testers across both tests; it needs other students' data on the relay, and V-35 says no leaderboard. The largest open request |
| A question in any free gap (LF-17) | Decision 5. One tester, on placement. Declared as a limit: the design assumes lectures |
| One sample question before setup (task 3, version A) | 3 of 4 preferred signing in first, which is what v2.0 built (V-45). The caveats in `lofi-results.md` section 4 stand |
| LF-8, LF-19, LF-20, LF-21 | About the lo-fi and how the test was run, not the app |

## 6. How it is built and checked

**Order:** A (words), B (the week), C (the block on the phone). Each part is committed by itself.

- **Tests first**, as tables of cases in `tests/companion-*.test.ts` and `tests/companion-relay*.test.ts`: the re-plan (which blocks move, where to, the daily limit, no room left, Undo), the block's round on the phone, each place's screen, and the renamed wording.
- **Each changed screen in Chrome**, the phone's at phone size against a relay running on the laptop, with real clicks.
- **One relay deploy** from Jiven's Cloudflare account, asked for first. It publishes the phone app's changes.
- **Jiven's iPhone**, from a short written checklist this time, so what was checked is on record.
- **Release:** PR `feat/v2.2` to `main`, CI green, squash merge, tag `lockedin-v2.2`. Decisions go in `DECISIONS.md` as V-71 onwards, and the version in `VERSIONS.md`.

**Owed to the group:** design-log rows for decisions 2 to 6, since each acts on a finding before T19 has agreed it.

**As built, beyond the tables above:**
- A block's questions are sent for today's and tomorrow's blocks only; later blocks get theirs nearer the day, so the week stays inside the relay's size limit.
- Answering on the move does not start the block, so the rest of it can still be moved. Answering all of them does.
- The phone remembers which of a block's questions it has asked, so a block moved after two answers carries on at the third.
- A place that is tapped again is un-picked.
- Undo for a re-plan lasts for the day it was made, and goes once any of the moved blocks is changed by hand.
- The laptop's help line under the timetable ("Drag a study block to any free time or day…") was replaced by the one above it.
