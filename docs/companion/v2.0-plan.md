# LockedIn v2.0: build plan

**Status:** approved by Jiven on 24 Nov 2026 ("fixes out first"): step 1 is released as `lockedin-v1.1.1`; steps 2 to 14 are built back to back and released together. **Design and reasons:** [`v2.0-direction.md`](v2.0-direction.md). **Decisions:** [`DECISIONS.md`](DECISIONS.md) V-39 to V-54. **Built on:** `lockedin-v1.1`, branch `feat/v2.0`.

This plan says what ships, in what order, and how each part is checked. It adds no design of its own: where the direction doc and the decisions are silent, section 3 lists the choice for approval.

---

## 1. What ships

Fourteen steps. Each is built tests first, checked in a real browser, and committed on its own.

### Step 1. Defects in v1.1 (released as `lockedin-v1.1.1`, 24 Nov)
| Defect | Fix |
|---|---|
| Nothing can start a study block, so block points and three milestones can't be earned | A Start button on the block (Week and Today), and the block notification opens the block's session. Either marks the block started (V-44) |
| "Stop for now" loses the resume card | A stopped session with questions left is offered on Today, with "Saved at question 3. Nothing is marked missed" |
| A right answer at the default 50% doesn't clear a gap | Four confidence taps, none preselected (brought forward from step 2) |
| A block's first step is chosen and never shown | Shown on the block and in its notification |

### Step 2. The session, finished to the lo-fi
- "Question 2 of 5" counts the question you are on, with dots.
- The slide beside a question is that question's own slide.
- End of a set: "Take a 5-min break", "3 more questions", "Finish for today". One buzz when the break ends.
- Done screen: no score; names the ideas that will come back and the next block.
- The phone's answer shows on the laptop's hand-off card: "Question 1: ✓ answered on your phone" or "not quite, it will come back".

### Step 3. Practise as a mode (V-40, V-50, V-51)
- A session has a mode, **Learn** or **Practise**, shown in its header and in the event log.
- Practise draws from the question bank with a mixing rule: across the module's ideas, some of each difficulty with the easiest first, weighted to gaps and fading ideas, and no question repeated until the others on its idea are used.
- It needs no AI and has no wait. Answer, four confidence taps, see why, with the slide.
- "Practise this module" on every module and on Today.
- **On the phone (V-46), built with step 10 so the relay is deployed once:** after the first question, "4 more on your laptop", **"Keep going here"** or "Done for now". "Keep going here" runs the rest of the round on the phone with "Another one" and "Save the rest for later". "Done for now" says "Saved. The other 4 wait for your next free gap. Nothing is overdue."

### Step 4. Gaps by idea (V-39, V-43)
- What you chose is recorded with every answer.
- A gap belongs to an idea, not to one question in one session.
- **Progress → Ideas to revisit:** module, then idea, then the questions missed. A question opens to what you chose, the right answer, why, and the slide.
- **"Practise this idea":** the AI writes 5 new questions around the gap, from the idea's slides, your missed questions, what you chose and how sure you were. Each cites its slide and is marked as written by AI. They are saved to the idea's bank. With no AI connected, the round uses unused bank questions on that idea.
- **An idea leaves the list** after two right answers on different questions, on different days, each "Fairly sure" or "Certain". Answers anywhere count.
- Question ids stay the same when a study pack is made again.

### Step 5. Learn (V-47, V-54)
- **Start:** pick a lecture, pick "this week", or type or say what you want and where you think you are.
- **Probe:** sized to that (a few high-level questions for a week, a couple for one section), "have a guess", never marked wrong, never a gap. "Skip, just teach me" is always there.
- **Plan:** the session's ideas as a picture, the current one lit, the ones passed marked. It stays beside the teaching.
- **The tutor's tools, switched on:** type or speak at any point; predict questions shown as guesses; written answers; hint; a worked example of a similar question; demos, drawn visuals and web research in the background, with a note that one is on its way.
- **Length:** as long as the block, not five questions. Stopping saves the session with its plan; it is offered again on Today and in the next block on that topic.
- **Long sessions keep their slides:** the slides for the idea being taught are supplied again as the plan reaches it.
- **A week in one session:** opens on the maps of that week's units instead of one unit's slides.

### Step 6. What LockedIn knows about you (V-52, V-53)
- **First Learn in a module:** "How do you like to learn this?" by tapping, typing or speaking. From the second module: "The same as my other modules". Skip, or "I don't know", gives the plain loop.
- **Three notes per module,** shown on the module's page, each editable: how you learn it; where you are in it (rewritten after each Learn session); the last session's plan and where it stopped.
- The tutor is handed all three when a session starts. "Delete all my data" removes them.

### Step 7. The first run (V-45)
1. Sign in with ChatGPT, or use another AI account. One button, no model pickers; models are chosen for you.
2. Your timetable: paste the link, your modules appear.
3. Your slides and notes: drop them in; they are prepared in the background.
4. Your week: "When do you have energy?", then blocks, each with its reason, ending on "Looks good".
5. Your phone: scan, type the code.

Every step but the first can be skipped with "Later". Setup ends on "Learn: {an idea from your own material}".

### Step 8. Today as one next step
- One line of context and one card: the next thing to do, in a named mode, with its reason.
- Shown only when relevant: the hand-off from the phone, a session to pick up, the Sunday check-in.
- Starter cards and counts go.

### Step 9. Week
- Start a block; the block names its first step and says why it is where it is.
- Remove with Undo. Add a block. A moved block keeps a "moved" label.
- Move by "next free gap" and three suggested times, as well as dragging.
- "Add blocks to my calendar".

### Step 10. The phone
- "Why now" in the notification itself.
- Four confidence taps on the phone's question screen.
- The block notification names the first step and opens the block on the laptop.
- "Next free gap" when moving a block.
- Answer buttons on the notification **only if** T18 prefers them to tap-to-open.

### Step 11. Visuals (V-33)
- The concept map and the session plan are drawn by LockedIn from the AI's data, in layers, coloured *solid / shaky / to revisit / not yet*, never by colour alone.
- Explainers as shapes the AI fills in: steps, a comparison, a matrix. Mermaid is the fallback.

### Step 12. Your courses
- One drop for everything, sorted by module and week using the timetable; anything unsure is asked once.
- A chapter can span several weeks.
- Week numbers match the university's teaching weeks.
- One line per unit: *ready*, *preparing*, *needs you*.
- A lecture opens on its map, with **Learn this** and **Practise this**.

### Step 13. Me and Advanced
Your AI, Phone, Notifications, Appearance, Add blocks to my calendar, What LockedIn uses, and **Advanced ▸** for models, thinking levels, voice key, calendar details, block length and routine.

### Step 14. Motion
The moments in the direction doc's section 13, at today's timings, off when the system asks for reduced motion.

## 4. Order, and what you see along the way

Steps 1 to 14, in that order. The order puts what is broken first, then the two modes, then what depends on them.

**One thing to decide with this plan.** You chose one release (V-42). The four defects in step 1 are in the build that testers will use at the hi-fi test, and step 1 is small. I recommend shipping step 1 by itself as `lockedin-v1.1.1` first: it is a bug-fix release, not a cut of v2.0, and everything after it still lands as one `lockedin-v2.0`.

**How large this is.** It is the largest release so far: two session modes, a new first run, new AI prompts, and changes to the phone app, which means one relay deploy from your Cloudflare account when step 10 is ready. It will not all be finished before the hi-fi test on 26 to 27 Nov. Whatever is on `main` that day is what testers see.

## 5. How it is checked

- **Rules first:** table-driven tests for every pure rule (the mixing rule, the gap rule, sizing the probe, the notes' caps, block start). `npm run companion:check`, typecheck and lint before each commit.
- **In a browser:** each screen driven with a real mouse and keyboard in Chrome on a copy of your data, and the result read back from the server, as in v1.1.
- **With the real AI:** Learn, the first-time questions, the notes and "Practise this idea" run against a copy of your data on your ChatGPT sign-in. I will report what the AI actually wrote, including where it was poor.
- **On your phone:** "Keep going here" and the block notification need you, after the relay deploy.
- **What I can't check:** whether students like it. That is T23.
