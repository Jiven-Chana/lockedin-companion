## 2. How the design was chosen

Four working samples that differ in what the student does, each on a numeric idea and a non-numeric one, with the evidence for and against each: [`explainer-designs/index.html`](explainer-designs/index.html) (PR #22).

| | What the student does | Chosen |
|---|---|---|
| A. Build it up | Steps through the picture at their own pace | Yes |
| B. Change it and see | Plays with a small model | No: only works where there are numbers; riskiest when the AI's model is wrong |
| C. Fill the gap | Guesses a missing block, then sees why | Yes, as A's first step |
| D. Two cases compared | Finds what differs between a mistake and the right version | No, for now |

A rests on AFF-C (Strong: "too much at once", one step at a time), INT-02-g, the look test, and two meta-analyses (Rey et al. 2019; Schroeder & Cenkci 2018). C answers A's weakness, that it is watching (Q-6, Strong; LIT-26). No student has seen either: the hi-fi test on 30 Nov is the first chance.

## 4. The plan

1. **Blocks only.** The tutor and the explainer writer are told to write a picture as plain boxes and arrows, which LockedIn draws. Mermaid's own drawing stays as the fallback for anything else.
2. **Steps.** The picture arrives a step at a time, with Back, Next and "Show it all". A step lights its blocks, can put a short note on a block, and has one sentence. The enlarged view steps too.
3. **The gap.** A picture can open with one gap: two or three blocks to choose from, a reason on each, and "Show me instead". Mint or lilac, nothing marked, nothing sent to the tutor.
4. **Where.** The tutor's pictures in Learn, and the Library's explainer cards for study packs made from now on. Explainers already made stay as they are: nothing is rewritten automatically (AI usage).
5. **How the AI supplies it.** Comment lines inside the diagram it already writes (`%% step`, `%% gap`). Missing or wrong, and the whole picture shows as before.
6. **Not in this version.** B and D.

## 5. As built

- The rules are `src/core/companion/picture.ts` (`parsePictureScript`, `pictureFrame`); the limits and the words are `PICTURE_RULES` and `PICTURE_COPY` in `policy.ts`; the screen is `components/lockedin/picture.tsx`.
- A block arrives at the first step that names it and stays. A block no step names is there from the start. One step alone is not a build-up: the picture is then shown whole.
- **Beyond the plan, found by measuring:** at his window (1470 x 750) the first build's picture card was 760 px tall, so the sentence and Next were below the fold. Now a picture's blocks are smaller than a concept map's (184 x 64), and the sentence and buttons sit beside the picture when the row has room, under it when not. The card is 562 px there. Enlarged, the picture grows to the window's height (up to 1.6 times), with the sentence beside it.
- "Show it all" lists every step's sentence beside the whole picture.
- A guess and a finished picture are logged (`picture_gap`, `picture_done`) for the study's records. Nothing reads them.

## 6. Checked

- 1,511 companion tests, written first for the rules.
- Every state of a picture (gap, guessed, steps, all, enlarged) at 1470 x 750, 1920 x 1000, 1336 x 680, 1176 x 600, 1024 x 700 and 735 x 700, with the probe for cut and spilling content. Chrome only.
- **The real AI, four calls.** One Learn session (3 tutor turns): asked for a picture of GAN training, the tutor wrote plain boxes with three steps and notes ("frozen", "update") at the first attempt, and it drew. One unit's explainers written again (1 call, 51 s): 8 of 8 are pictures that build up, each with four steps and a gap, none rejected. Before, 0 of that unit's 8 diagrams could be drawn by LockedIn; all went to mermaid.

## 7. Not checked, and what it costs

- **A gap written by the tutor in Learn.** The tutor's picture had steps and no gap (a gap is its choice). The gap was seen working on the explainer cards and on a hand-written picture.
- Dark mode, high contrast, Motion off, and the keyboard alone, on a picture.
- Whether students learn more from it: not known.
- **AI usage.** A picture's steps and gap are about 150 tokens more than the bare diagram (measured on the 8 explainers: 153 to 753 characters each), so about 1,200 more tokens for a unit's study pack, and about 150 for each picture the tutor draws.
- Diagrams written left to right (`graph LR`) are still left to mermaid (V-84); the prompts now ask for `graph TD`.
