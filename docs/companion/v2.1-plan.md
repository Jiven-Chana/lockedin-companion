## 2. The phone, approved 25 Nov

### A. After the lecture (lo-fi task 1, version B)

| # | What | Lo-fi wording |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | The notification carries its reason | **"HCI just finished. One question:"**, the question, then "Why now: lecture ended, you're free till 15:00" |
| A2 | The question screen | Header "LockedIn" with "HCI · Lecture 7"; "Question 1 of 5"; the question; three options |
| A3 | The result | **"✓ Right."** or **"Not quite: {the right option}."**, the explanation, and a tag "Lecture 7 · slide 12" |
| A4 | Three ways on | "4 more on your laptop" (the main button), "Keep going here" (V-46), "Done for now" |
| A5 | Keep going here | Header "{n} of 5 done". After each answer: "{n} of 5 done. Up to you." with "Another one" (main) and "Save the rest for later". After the fifth: "That's all 5" |
| A6 | Saved | **"Saved."** "The other 4 questions wait for your next free gap. Nothing is overdue." |

**What "wait for your next free gap" does (Jiven, 25 Nov):** the saved questions are one card on the phone's home screen ("4 questions saved", "Carry on"), and they go first in the next question notification that was already going to be sent. No extra notification is added (R-1, R-6).

The week's reason line ("Blocks at 14:00 because you have energy in the afternoon.") uses each block's own reason from v2.0 (`StudyBlock.placed`).

### Choices made for you, approved with the plan

| Choice | What is built |
|---|---|
| The three move times | The first free time later today, this evening and tomorrow morning. "Next free gap" is the earliest free time of all. The laptop works them out and sends them with each block |
| The block's goal line | A Practise block: "5 questions, about 10 min". A Learn block: what it teaches and the block's length |
| Nudges per day set on the phone | 0 stops notifications at once, even with the laptop closed. A change between 1 and 2 takes effect when the laptop next syncs |
| Numbers and wording | All in `policy.ts`; the phone app's own strings in its `COPY` |

### Not in this part

- **Waiting for T18's results:** answering on the lock screen (version A; from what could be found, an iPhone web app cannot show answer buttons on a notification), "Where are you?" and with it "On the move" from a block, focus levels and the shield, one sample question before setup.
- **Left out:** the lo-fi Settings' calendar and leaderboard switches and "Advanced" (T18 does not test them; the leaderboard is V-35), and confidence taps on the phone (the lo-fi has none, so a phone answer still can't close a gap, V-43).

### How it is built and checked

- Tests first: the saved round, the move times, the relay moving a block's notification, nudges set to 0, each as a table of cases (`tests/companion-*.test.ts`, `tests/companion-relay*.test.ts`).
- Each phone screen in Chrome at phone size against a relay running on the laptop.
- One relay deploy from Jiven's Cloudflare account, asked for first, then his iPhone.
