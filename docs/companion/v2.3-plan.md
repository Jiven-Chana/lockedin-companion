# LockedIn v2.3: a phone you can use by yourself

**Status:** approved by Jiven on 27 Nov 2026 ("Approve, build it"), with the three decisions in section 1. **Built on:** `lockedin-v2.2`, branch `feat/v2.3`.

**What v2.3 is for:** after running the v2.2 checklist on his iPhone, Jiven found the phone too thin: "we literally have 2 features, a notification, a crappy calander, a move button and a looks good button". A block in the week could not be opened, nothing could be started without a notification, and maths would show as raw text. He also asked that "work done on the phone also shows on the laptop".

**Why the phone was thin:** the group decided on 16 Nov that the phone starts and the laptop teaches, and v2.1 built only what the lo-fi tests. The lo-fi test then came out 2 of 4 for the laptop (LF-3), and one tester does question-bank questions on his phone on the bus (P04). A phone that can be opened and used by itself now has evidence behind it.

| # | Decision | Chosen over |
|---|---|---|

## 2. What is built

### A. Open a block from the week

| # | What | Wording and behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | A block's line in the week is a button | Tapping it opens the block's screen. Move and Remove stay on the row |
| A2 | A block on its own day | The screen v2.2 has: goal, first step, "Where are you? (optional)", "Take the first step", "Not now" |
| A3 | A block on another day | Goal, first step, why it is there, and "This block is on Sat at 14:00." Buttons "Move it" and "Back". No start buttons: a block starts on its own day (V-44) |
| A4 | A way back | Every block screen has "Back", which returns to the home screen |

### B. Practise from the home screen

| # | What | Wording and behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | The card | **"Practise"**, "Five questions, any time.", then a button per module that has questions on the phone |
| B2 | A round | Header "Practise" with "HCI · 2 of 5 done". The question, then the result as now ("✓ Right." or "Not quite: …", the explanation, the slide). "Another one" (main) and "Done for now". After the fifth: "That's all 5" |
| B3 | A module with nothing left | Its button goes. With none left anywhere: "You've answered every question on this phone. More arrive when LockedIn is next open on your laptop." |
| B4 | No study packs yet | No card |

### C. Maths on the phone

| # | What | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Maths is drawn, not printed | Anything between `$…$`, `$$…$$` or `\(…\)` in a question, its options or its explanation is drawn as maths on every phone question screen. Works offline |
| C2 | Markup that can't be drawn | Shows as it was typed, as today |

The laptop already draws maths in questions; this brings the phone level with it.

### D. The laptop shows what the phone did

| # | What | Wording and behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | A line on Today | "On your phone today: 4 questions, HCI and Game Theory." ("1 question, HCI.") Only on a day with phone answers. Never how many were right (R-6) |
| D2 | A unit practised only on the phone | No longer reads "not started" in the Library, on Progress or to the week planner |

## 3. Choices made for you, to approve with the plan

| Choice | What is built | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Which questions the phone holds | Ten per module, chosen by the laptop's own Practise rule: mixed across ideas, weighted to gaps, least-asked first. Chosen again at every upload | One rule for Practise on both devices (V-40) |
| When they refresh | When the laptop next syncs. The phone never asks the same question twice from one pool | The phone has no AI and no slides; the laptop holds the packs |
| "Done for now" in phone Practise | Ends the round. Nothing is saved for later: the rest of the pool is still there | It was the student's own choice to start, so nothing is owed |
| What a phone Practise answer counts for | As any phone answer: the score, a wrong one becomes a gap, none can close a gap | Decision 1 |
| The relay's size limit | Raised so the pool fits; the laptop trims the pool to whatever limit the relay reports, so an older relay still works | As v2.2 did for a block's questions |
| Where the maths drawing comes from | The same library the laptop uses (KaTeX), copied into the phone app when the relay is deployed. Nothing is loaded from another site | Offline use, and no third party sees the phone |
| The Today line | Counts and module names only | R-6 |

## 4. Not in v2.3

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Carrying on a half-done round on the other device | Decision 3. Known gaps that stay: answers given on the move do not carry into the block's session on the laptop, and questions saved on the phone do not show on the laptop |
| A faster check between laptop and phone | Decision 3. The laptop hears from the phone once a minute while LockedIn is open, and when it is next opened otherwise |
| Confidence taps on the phone | Decision 1 |
| The week as a calendar, and the phone's look | v3.0, with the look chosen by the look test |
| Learn on the phone | The tutor runs on the laptop's AI sign-in |

## 5. How it is built and checked

**Order:** A, C, D, then B (the largest). Each part is committed by itself.

- **Tests first**, as tables of cases: which questions go in a module's pool, the pool trimmed to the relay's limit, the Today line's wording, a unit's standing after phone answers, and each new phone screen run in a simulated phone.
- **Each changed screen in Chrome**, the phone's at phone size against a relay running on the laptop.
- **One relay deploy** from Jiven's Cloudflare account, asked for first.
- **Jiven's iPhone**, from a written checklist.
- **Release:** PR `feat/v2.3` to `main`, CI green, squash merge, tag `lockedin-v2.3`. Decisions go in `DECISIONS.md` as V-76 onwards.

**As built, beyond the tables above:**
- The phone forgets what it has asked from a module when the laptop sends a different pool, so the laptop decides what comes back.
- With room for only part of the pools, questions come off the largest pool first.
- A notification cannot draw maths: a question with maths shows its markup on the lock screen and is drawn once opened.
- Units answered on the phone before v2.3 count too: the log is read once.
- A formula the library cannot draw shows as it was typed.
