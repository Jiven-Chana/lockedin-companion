# LockedIn v3.0: the Blocks look

**Status:** the decisions in section 2 were made by Jiven on 27 Nov 2026. The plan as a whole is waiting for his approval. **Built on:** `lockedin-v2.3`, branch `feat/v3.0`.

**What v3.0 is for:** the look test (26 and 27 Nov) showed four complete looks for the same screens. Every tester chose Blocks for the whole app. v3.0 rebuilds the laptop app and the phone app in Blocks, changed where testers said Blocks was wrong.

## 3. One system

Everything below is defined once, in `src/core/companion/`, and used by the laptop, the phone and the diagrams.

### Colour: each colour means one thing

| Colour | Means | Never used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Mint** | Right, secured, done | A module |
| **Lilac** | Your pick when it was not the answer; an idea to revisit | A module. Never red anywhere |
| **A module's colour** | That module: its panels, its study blocks, its mark | Right or wrong |
| **Ink, paper, card, sand** | Text and outlines; the page; reading surfaces; grid lines and busy time | — |

- **Module colours, in order:** tangerine, rose, sky blue, lime, aqua, orchid. A seventh module starts the list again. Yellow and a calmer set can be picked in Me.
- **A change from what was approved:** decision 3's list had leaf green and violet. They sit too close to mint and lilac, so a module could look "right" or "to revisit". They are replaced by lime and orchid.
- **State is never colour alone:** a shape and a word go with every state, as in the Blocks style sheet.
- **Text on a colour is always ink,** in light and in dark.

### Type, shape, icons, motion

| Part | What |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Bricolage Grotesque 800 for headings and numerals; Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading. Both are in the app already |
| **Shape** | Thick ink outlines and rounded corners. **A hard shadow means it can be pressed;** a flat panel only shows something |
| **Buttons** | One ink button per screen is the next step. Every other action has the pressable look too: a tester missed the "quiet" ones ("Practise instead", "Show me first", slide links) |
| **Icons** | Filled pictograms, always beside a word. A module has a simple shape (circle, triangle, square, diamond, hexagon, star) with its colour, since LockedIn cannot know a pictogram for every course |
| **Menu** | A tab bar across the top; the current tab joins the page |
| **Motion** | A pressable thing lifts on hover and lands when pressed. The map's ideas arrive in order once. Nothing moves with "reduce motion" on |

## 5. Diagrams drawn by LockedIn

| Diagram | How it is drawn |
|---|---|
| **A lecture's concept map** | Rows of blocks. An idea sits one row below everything it builds on. Links are straight with square corners, and a link that skips a row has a slot of its own. Each idea is a button with its standing as a shape and a word (Secured, Getting there, To revisit, Not yet), and its slides ("Slide 24 +9") |
| **A Learn session's plan** | The same blocks in a row: done, now, to come |
| **A diagram in a tutor's answer or an explainer** | Drawn by LockedIn when it is plain boxes and arrows. Anything else stays with mermaid, in the same colours |

**Making the arrows hold.** The layout is a pure rule with its own tests, run on every concept map in the repository and on 500 generated ones:

- No two blocks overlap.
- No link passes through a block that is not one of its ends.
- Every link in the data is drawn. A link that would make a loop is drawn last and marked, never dropped silently.
- The same map always gives the same drawing.
- A map too wide for its space, or on a narrow screen, falls back to one column in study order, with no lines to break.
- A map that cannot be read at all shows the list of ideas, as today.

## 7. Choices made for you, to approve with the plan

| Choice | What is built | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Getting there" on a map | Read as the "progress bar on each topic" Jiven liked: a part-filled mint edge. It shows a state, not a percentage | No measure of "how far" exists per idea beyond the four states |
| The time-of-day background | Removed | Blocks has one page colour in light and one in dark |
| A student who has picked a theme colour | It is not used any more; their light, dark, text size and spacing settings carry over | Decision 2 |
| The phone's week covers | Today and the six days after it, not Monday to Sunday | The phone is only sent the days ahead |
| High contrast | Pure black and white outlines and text, no soft tints | The existing setting, kept |
| Colours in code | A test fails if a colour is written anywhere outside the one definitions file, on the laptop or the phone | "Ensuring colours are unanimous across the system" |

## 9. How it is built and checked

**Stages, each committed by itself:**

1. **The system:** colours in light and dark, type, shapes, buttons, tags, icons, the tab bar.
2. **Today.**
3. **Practise and the answer.**
4. **Learn.**
5. **Progress.**
6. **Week.**
7. **Diagrams.**
8. **Library, a lecture, setup, connecting an AI, Me.**
9. **The phone.**
10. **Motion, docs, release.**

- **Tests first** for every rule: the layout, the colour meanings, contrast of ink on every colour in light and dark, the module colour order.
- **Every screen in Chrome, in light and in dark,** on a copy of Jiven's data, with pictures kept.
- **A check after stage 2:** Jiven sees Today in light and dark before the other screens are built.
- **One relay deploy** for the phone, asked for first, then his iPhone.
- **Release:** PR `feat/v3.0` to `main`, CI green, squash merge, tag `lockedin-v3.0`. Decisions go in `DECISIONS.md` as V-80 onwards.

**Owed to the group:** the third tester's session written into the team repo, and design-log rows for decisions 1 to 7.
