# LockedIn v1.0: what to fix before it ships

**Status:** issue list, 24 Nov 2026. Not a plan yet: the plan comes after the decisions in section 10 are made.

**Where these come from:** Jiven's own testing on 23 and 24 Nov (real ChatGPT sign-in, his Birmingham timetable, his Game Theory and Neural Computation notes, his phone). Each issue was reproduced from his data folders (`runs/try`, `runs/mine`) or by running the app, unless it is marked *suspected*.

**What v1.0 means:** v0.1 proved the pieces work one at a time. v1.0 makes them work together, for a student's real material, smoothly enough to package and hand out. It fixes every bug below, removes clutter in the app and the code, and ships as a desktop app.

Each issue has an id, **what you see**, **why** (the cause, if found), and a **direction** (not a decision).

---

## 1. Teaching sessions

### T-1. A session on a new lecture never asks question 1 (blocker)
- **What you see:** "Something got stuck" when opening a session on your own notes.
- **Why:** this is verified twice: Game Theory intro (23 Nov) and Neural Computation (24 Nov), both `no_question`. After setting its plan (`plan_init`), the teacher writes "The plan is set. We'll start by…" and ends its turn without asking anything. The 23 Nov safety net ("Next question, please.") only runs between questions, not before the first.
- **Direction:** extend the safety net to the preparing phase, and remove the plan presentation from the doctrine for app sessions. Re-run the full checkpoint (`CHECKPOINT.md`) on two real lectures before v1.0.

### T-2. The probe → predict → learn → check loop is invisible
- **What you see:** sessions look like a plain quiz. Nothing shows that the app first finds what you know, then teaches one step, then checks it.
- **Why:** the engine already runs the loop. Questions are tagged `predict` or `check`, and a probe phase runs first. LockedIn's session screen shows every question the same way. The team's research names this loop as the core of the product (`research/literature-learning-loop.md`, LIT-24 to LIT-31).
- **Direction:** split two modes clearly:
  - **Teach:** the loop, made visible (probe, then learn, predict and check per idea), for a lecture you haven't learned yet.
  - **Practice:** quick questions from the slides and question bank, for revision.
  
  A diagram of the lecture's ideas can show where you are in the loop without extra screens (see V-3).

### T-3. Long decks (80+ slides) don't fit
- **What you see:** the Neural Computation deck is 79 slides. Sessions on it failed (T-1), and the slide panel is a long strip to flick through.
- **Why (suspected for the teacher part):** the opening message trims slides to a token budget (`PREPARE_RULES.slideTokenBudget`), so on a long deck the teacher may only see the start. Not yet confirmed on this deck.
- **Direction:** teach long decks idea by idea: the concept map's nodes become the session's scope, each with only its own slides. Show the panel by idea, not by page number.

### T-4. Several study packs at once fail, with a misleading error
- **What you see:** 5 of your 6 Game Theory packs failed. Four said "chatgpt catalog has no model gpt-6-sol", one said "ChatGPT request failed".
- **Why:** this is verified. All five started in the same second (23 Nov 22:15). Running them in parallel on one ChatGPT sign-in fails. The catalog error is a symptom (the model list didn't load), not the cause. Nothing retries.
- **Direction:** queue packs one at a time, retry a failed stage automatically, and say in plain words what happened ("ChatGPT was busy, trying again").

### T-5. A one-page unit can't get a pack
- **What you see:** "Week 8" Neural Computation (one page) failed with "the model never called submit_map".
- **Direction:** below a minimum size, don't make a separate unit. Merge it into its neighbour, or make practice questions only.

### T-6. "Something got stuck" doesn't say what or what to do
- **Direction:** every failure screen names the cause in plain words and offers one action: try again, use practice questions, or pick another lecture.

## 2. Slides and notes

### S-1. The slide panel shows "No material" on every page of uploaded notes (blocker)
- **Why:** this is verified on your Game Theory unit. Pages keep their original numbers (this unit is pages 18 to 38), but the panel counts 1, 2, 3… and finds nothing at those numbers.
- **Direction:** the panel moves through the unit's own pages. It shows "Page 18 of 38", or better, the idea being taught (T-3).

### S-2. Slides render as raw extracted text, with garbled maths
- **What you see:** for example, "𝒙 ∈ ℝ!"∗!"" for 28 × 28, every label on its own line, no KaTeX, no structure.
- **Why:** the panel shows the text pulled out of the PDF. PDF text extraction loses layout, superscripts and maths fonts.
- **Direction:** show the real page. Render the PDF page as an image (already on the phase B list: "a PDF page view in the slide panel"). Keep the text only for search and for the AI. Optionally, the AI writes a clean one-screen summary per idea with KaTeX, which is what explainers already do well.

### S-3. Lecture notes are treated like slides
- **What you see:** a full-semester notes PDF becomes "slides" of long prose (about 2,500 characters each), units titled by chapter, all in "Week 0".
- **Direction:** recognise notes versus slides when uploading. Notes are split by chapter or section into ideas, not pages. Ask which week each chapter belongs to, or skip weeks entirely for revision material.

### S-4. Adding notes isn't smooth
- **What you see:**
  - Several files *can* be picked at once (Cmd-click, or drag several in), but nothing says so.
  - After saving, each unit's study pack must be started by hand.
  - Units with no week appear as "Week 0".
- **Direction:** one drop zone that says "drop all your files". After saving, write the packs in the background automatically (queued, see T-4). Say "week not set" with a one-tap fix instead of "Week 0".

## 3. Progress and visuals

### V-1. Progress page layout
- **What you see:** "Week 0" rows; status pills that wrap onto two lines ("Not / yet", "To / revisit"); one module's card painted fully in its colour while others aren't.
- **Direction:** fix the layout; one consistent card style.

### V-2. Demo content mixes with real content
- **What you see:** with the sample timetable and your own uploads together, the bundled demo "Games in normal form" sits next to your "Games in Normal Form".
- **Direction:** the sample is for the demo only. Once a real calendar or upload exists, hide the sample units (or offer to remove them).

### V-3. Progress is lists, not a picture
- **Direction:** use the concept map per lecture as the progress view (ideas coloured solid, shaky, to revisit, not yet), and a module-level diagram of lectures. One diagram replaces several screens.

## 4. Settings and clutter in the app

### C-1. Me is long and technical
- **What you see:** ten sections. Model slots and "thinking" levels sit next to text size.
- **Direction:** keep a short Me (your AI connected or not, phone, notifications, appearance, your data). Move model choice, voice keys and calendar details behind "Advanced".

### C-2. The plain address shows LearnHarness
- **What you see:** `localhost:3000` shows the engine's own screens. LockedIn is at `/app`.
- **Direction:** redirect `/` to `/app` (already planned for the desktop app, F-117).

## 5. Phone

### P-1. Installing on an iPhone isn't seamless (blocker for T4)
- **What you see:** scanning the QR code opens a page with one sentence of instructions and nothing else. It's not clear how to "Add to Home Screen".
- **Why:** iPhones only allow notifications from web apps on the Home Screen, so the page waits until it's installed.
- **Direction:**
  - a guided install screen with pictures of the Share button and "Add to Home Screen";
  - a preview of a question so the page isn't empty;
  - a check that it worked ("✓ Installed. Now tap Allow notifications").
  
  On Android and desktop browsers, skip the step.

### P-2. A real notification hasn't been confirmed yet
- **What's known:** your phone paired with the live relay on 24 Nov 13:42. Whether a notification arrived hasn't been checked.
- **Direction:** test on iPhone and Android before v1.0, including a locked phone with the laptop shut.

### P-3. The relay is on a personal Cloudflare account
- **Direction:** move it to a team account (decision O-1) before handing out the app. Moving changes the address, so everyone pairs again.

### P-4. Demo clock versus real time
- The relay sends on real time. While a demo moves the clock, the phone's own nudges land on the wrong day. "Send a question to the phone now" works around it for tests. Fine for v1.0 if demo mode stays facilitator-only.

## 6. Calendar

### K-1. Canvas deadlines would be read as lectures
- **Why:** a timed Canvas event whose title names a module would become a zero-length "lecture", and LockedIn would nudge "HCI just finished" at midnight.
- **Direction:** never treat zero-length or "due" events as lectures. Later, support a second "Deadlines" link that the planner uses (a new feature, needs a decision).

### K-2. Birmingham's timetable: fixed 24 Nov
- The link from myUoB now reads cleanly (6 modules, 60 lectures in Jiven's timetable), and onboarding explains where to find it. Listed here so v1.0 keeps a test for it.

## 7. Platform and developer experience

### D-1. Dev mode can sit on a loading screen
- **What you see:** "Checking your AI connection…" for a minute or more.
- **Why (partly found):** right after a dev server starts or code changes, Next compiles and reloads the page repeatedly. The exact trigger wasn't pinned down. A production build doesn't have this.
- **Direction:** testers and demos always use a production build (and, in v1.0, the desktop app).

### D-2. Next.js security warning
- Next prints a "known security vulnerability" warning on start. An earlier upgrade broke packaging tests and was reverted. Upgrade deliberately in v1.0, with the packaging tests as the gate.

## 8. Code clutter and integrity

*(From a read-only audit of the companion code on 24 Nov. The three most serious items were re-checked by hand.)*

### X-1. The event log double-counts (corrupts T23 data)
- `session_opened` is logged several times for one session. The page asks for updates every 750 ms while preparing, and each request sees the session as not yet opened (`service.ts` `view()` passes an old copy to `markOpened`). Seen: 4 identical events within 20 ms in `runs/try`. *(Re-checked.)*
- Every check-in answer also logs `checkin_shown` (`answerCheckin` calls `checkin()` without `quiet`), and so does every check-in replayed from the phone. *(Re-checked.)*
- **The same action is logged twice under different names:**
  - saving material logs `settings_changed` from the browser and `materials_saved` from the server;
  - `setup_chat_fallback_form` is logged in two places;
  - a check-in target change logs `target_changed {via: "settings"}`, which is the wrong `via`.
- **One event type has several shapes:** `onboarding_done` (v0.0 vs v0.1), `block_started` (2), `settings_changed` (4).
- **Phone and laptop name the same plan action differently:** `plan_tick` versus `plan_accepted`.
- `engine_ready` is logged on every server restart.
- *Possible race (not verified):* two overlapping updates could both send "Next question, please." and log `teacher_nudged` twice.
- **Direction:**
  - log each event at the single place its state changes, from the fresh record;
  - one shape per event type;
  - one name per action;
  - a test that one user action produces exactly one event.

### X-2. Dead code from v0.0
- **The v0.0 onboarding path** (`onboard()`, its own module-code rule, the unused `lockedIn.onboard`) is reached only by tests, the tracer and the demo scenarios.
- **The simulated phone lock, rejected by V-8:**
  - `appOpen`, `activeBlock`, `skipBlock`, `lockDecision`;
  - the routes `blocks/app-open`, `blocks/active` and `blocks/[id]/skip`;
  - `prefs.lock`, the `BLOCK_RULES` focus/break/override numbers, and the `app_open_blocked` and `lock_override` events.

  None of this is used by the UI.
- **Routes with no UI caller:** `roadmap`, `timetable/subscribe`, `blocks` GET/PUT, `sessions/[id]/cost`, `materials/[id]/pdf`, `nudges/schedule` (used by the phone link internally, not the browser).
- **`BLOCK_RULES` (v0.0) overlaps `PLAN_RULES`.** Block length can be 10–120 min in one place and 25–90 in another. `PACK_STAGES` is unused and in the wrong order.
- **Direction:** delete the lock and the v0.0 onboarding, move tests to the v0.1 paths, and keep one set of block rules.

### X-3. The project's own rules are broken in places
- **Engine internals are imported outside `lh-port.ts`:** `service.ts`, `study-pack.ts`, `default.ts`, `store.ts`, `engines/learnharness.ts`. The lint doesn't enforce this rule. The relay's lint zone also allows all of `src/core`, not just `src/core/companion`.
- **Numbers outside `policy.ts`:**
  - the weekly target range (twice);
  - default quiet hours (4 times);
  - "19:00" and 50 minutes repeated from `DEFAULT_PLAN`;
  - the banner lifetime (twice).
- **Wording outside `policy.ts`:**
  - "Reading your … slides → writing question 1" (3 times) and "Writing question N…" (twice);
  - several service error messages;
  - the phone app's copies of `HANDOFF_COPY.relayHolds` and `PLAN_COPY.feelings`;
  - the phone event types written twice.
- **Direction:**
  - route every engine import through `lh-port.ts`, and make the lint enforce it and the relay rule;
  - move numbers and wording into `policy.ts`;
  - generate the phone app's wording from `policy.ts` at build time.

### X-4. `service.ts` is 1,745 lines doing twelve jobs
- **What's in it:** the event log, profile, onboarding (two versions), calendar, course material, packs, the week plan, phone hand-off, the dead lock, nudges, sessions, Today and progress, and the facilitator.
- **Direction:** split it along its existing section banners into onboarding, material, calendar, plan, phone, nudges, sessions and demo. Move the inline input checks into pure validators in `src/core/companion`. Splitting out the phone part also removes a circular dependency.

### X-5. Smaller duplication and stale text
- **"Monday of this week" is written 5 times,** although `startOfWeek` exists in `time.ts`.
- **The nudge-type list is written 3 times.**
- **iCalendar parsing is set up twice.**
- **Misplaced comments:**
  - two doc comments are stacked on one function in `service.ts`;
  - the `TEACHER_RULES` comment sits above `PHONE_RULES`;
  - the `blocks.ts` header still describes the lock;
  - `VERSIONS.md` cites line numbers that moved.
- **Two "draft, group to confirm" markers remain** in `policy.ts`.

## 9. Packaging and distribution

- **N-0. The build output contains participant data (blocker, fix first).** `.next/standalone` includes `runs/`, about 1 MB of event logs from test sessions, plus `tests/`, `docs/`, `dist/` and `relay/`. *(Re-checked.)* `scripts/build-server.mjs` only refuses `data/` and `auth.json`. Add `runs/**` to the tracing excludes and to the refusal check before anything is packaged.
- **N-1. No desktop app yet (blocker for shipping).** F-117 was phase B. What's there is still LearnHarness's:
  - `electron-builder.yml` has `appId com.learnharness.LearnHarness`, `productName LearnHarness`, and publishes to the engine owner's repo *(re-checked)*;
  - `electron/main.cjs` stores data in LearnHarness's folder, so it would clash with an installed LearnHarness, and opens `/`, the engine's home;
  - `package.json` hard-codes the engine owner's Apple team id and notary profile, and keeps the engine's version, author and repo;
  - no `COMPANION_*` setting reaches the packaged app, so it would have no phone link.

  v1.0 needs:
  - downloadable Mac (DMG) and Windows (EXE) builds;
  - the LockedIn name, icon and identity, with its own data folder;
  - the app opening `/app`;
  - the relay address built in;
  - auto-update off;
  - the engine's own pages left out of the package or unreachable.
- **N-2. Unsigned builds** show macOS and Windows warnings. Either explain them on the download page, or sign them (that needs an Apple developer account).
- **N-3. Each student needs their own AI.** A ChatGPT sign-in or an API key. Check which ChatGPT plans the sign-in works with before telling testers what they need.

## 10. Decisions needed before planning v1.0

1. **Teach versus Practice** (T-2): two modes as described? What does each one start from (a lecture, an idea on the map, a revisit)?
2. **Slides** (S-2): show the real PDF page, an AI-cleaned summary, or both?
3. **Long decks** (T-3): teach by idea instead of by page?
4. **Notes versus slides** (S-3): split notes by chapter or idea, and do weeks matter for notes?
5. **Me** (C-1): which settings stay on the main page?
6. **Phone install** (P-1): is a guided Home Screen install acceptable, or should the phone part wait for a native app (out of scope for v0.1)?
7. **Relay account** (P-3): which team account, and who owns it?
8. **Deadlines** (K-1): add Canvas deadlines in v1.0 or later?
9. **Signing** (N-2): unsigned with instructions, or pay for signing?
10. **Scope and date:** v1.0 before or after the poster (2 Dec) and deck (4 Dec)?
