# LockedIn v1.1: progress that means something, and a week you can move

**Status:** approved by Jiven, 24 Nov 2026. Decisions V-34 to V-38 in [`DECISIONS.md`](DECISIONS.md).

**Where it comes from:** Jiven's test of v1.0 on his own six modules. Three decisions are already taken (24 Nov): a cumulative learning score with a view of what each module contributed, milestones you keep, and a real timetable grid with drag and drop. The evidence is in [`v1.1-research.md`](v1.1-research.md).

**In one line:** Progress stops being a list of weeks and becomes the history of what you have learned, with a reason to come back; the Week page becomes a timetable you can rearrange by hand.

---

## 1. What is wrong today

Checked in Jiven's data folder.

- **Progress repeats the Library.** It lists every lecture by week: 67 rows for his five modules.
- **Most of it is empty.** 59 of those rows are timetable lectures with no material, all "Not yet".
- **The weeks are doubled, and one set is wrong.** Game Theory shows his 7 chapters (weeks 1 to 7) and 11 timetable lectures also numbered 1 to 7. The timetable's numbers are not teaching weeks: "week 1" is the first week the calendar import could see.
- **Nothing shows change over time,** and nothing rewards learning or keeping to a plan.
- **The Week page has no time scale.** A study block can be moved with a time picker, but you can't see the free gap you are moving it into, and nothing stops it landing on a lecture.
- **A study block doesn't belong to a module** in a way you can see or change, so time can't be read per subject.

## 2. Progress, redesigned

### 2.1 The learning score

One number that builds up as you learn. It is worked out from the history the app already keeps (every answer, with its time, idea and question), so it is there for past work from the first day.

| What you did | Points |
|---|---|
| A right answer | 10, 15 or 20 by the question's difficulty (15 when the teacher wrote the question) |
| The first right answer on an idea ("idea secured") | +20 |
| A right answer on an idea that was waiting in "to revisit" ("came back") | +25 |
| Starting a study block you planned | +5 |
| A wrong answer, "I don't know", or "show me first" | 0. The idea goes to "to revisit", as today. Nothing is taken away |
| An idea you secured and haven't touched for 21 days ("fading") | −5 while it is fading. One right answer brings it back |

- **Honesty is never punished.** "I don't know" and a wrong answer cost nothing.
- **Fading is small and reversible.** It is the only way the score goes down.
- **Every number is tunable** in `policy.ts`.

### 2.2 The chart

- **The line is your score over time,** with three ranges: **Week** (by day), **Month** (by day), **Term** (by week).
- **A straight "steady pace" line** runs from your first day to the end of term. Its top is what your whole course is worth: every idea in your study packs, secured once. Add more material and it rises.
- **The end of term** is your last timetabled lecture, or 12 weeks from your first day when there is no timetable.
- **The gap to the line is never printed as a number,** and the page never says "behind" (see the research note, section 2).
- **Tap or hover any point** to see what happened that day: the sessions, how many you got right, which ideas became secured or came back, the blocks you kept, any milestone earned.
- **Milestones are marked on the line** where you earned them.
- **Drawn by LockedIn** as its own SVG, in your theme, with a table of the same figures for screen readers.

### 2.3 Where it came from

- **A bar per module:** the points each one contributed in the range shown, and the time you spent on it (sessions, and study blocks you started).
- **A module with nothing in the range is shown empty,** not hidden.

### 2.4 Needs attention

One card, at most three lines, each with one button.

- **The module you have done least on** in the last two weeks, among those with material: "Nothing on Game Theory for 9 days" → **5 questions on Zero-sum games**. The unit is the one the pacing rule would pick next.
- **Ideas that are fading:** "3 ideas fading in Neural Computation" → **Get them back**.
- **Modules with no material:** "No slides or notes yet for HCI, LALP and RAC" → **Add material**.

"Ideas to revisit" stays, below, ordered so the neglected module comes first.

### 2.5 Milestones

Earned from your history, dated, kept for good. None needs an unbroken chain.

| Learning | Discipline | Well-rounded |
|---|---|---|
| First idea secured | First study block kept | Two modules in one week |
| 10, 25 and 50 ideas secured | A full week kept (every block you accepted, started) | Every module with material in one week |
| A whole unit secured | Three full weeks kept (any three) | Material added for every module |
| Came back (first idea recovered from "to revisit") | First Sunday check-in | |
| 10 comebacks | First question answered on your phone | |

Earned ones show their date. The next few are shown faintly with what earns them.

### 2.6 What goes

- **The roadmap list of weeks.** The Library is the place for lists.
- **The three counters** (sessions, questions, ideas ready) at the top: the chart and "Where it came from" replace them.

## 3. The Week page as a timetable

- **A grid:** days across, hours down. Lectures, other classes and your routine's commitments are drawn at their real times and can't be moved.
- **Your study blocks are drawn in their module's colour** and can be dragged to any free time or another day. They snap to 15 minutes.
- **A block can't be dropped** on a lecture, a class, a commitment, another block or your quiet hours. It shows why and goes back.
- **The server checks the same rule,** so a block can't be put somewhere impossible by any route. Today it checks nothing.
- **Moving a suggested block keeps it,** as now.
- **Each block has a module.** The planner sets it from the unit it picked. Tap the module on a block to change it; the block then studies that module's next unit. "Free study" stays possible.
- **Keyboard:** a focused block moves with the arrow keys (15 minutes, or a day) and Enter drops it.
- **Narrow screens** keep today's list, where dragging has no room.
- **Not in v1.1:** resizing a block by dragging its edge, and adding a block by clicking an empty slot.

## 4. Choices made for you

| # | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Version:** v1.1, tagged `lockedin-v1.1`. v2.0 stays the name for the whole polish stage | This is its first slice, shipped on its own |
| 2 | **The score reads from the event log** and is worked out when the page opens | No new store; past work counts at once; "Delete all my data" already clears it |
| 3 | **Points:** the table in 2.1 | Learning earns far more than discipline; nothing is lost for being wrong |
| 4 | **Fading after 21 days, −5** | Long enough not to nag inside a normal fortnight; small beside +20 |
| 5 | **Steady pace ends at your last timetabled lecture** | The app already knows it; no setup question |
| 6 | **"Least done" looks back 14 days** | A week is too short for a module taught once a week |
| 7 | **15 milestones** (the table in 2.5), none time-chained | Enough to notice, few enough to mean something |
| 8 | **The timetable's wrong week numbers are hidden, not fixed.** Progress no longer lists timetable lectures, which is the only place they showed | Renumbering touches the pacing rule; it belongs with "Your courses" in v2.0 |
| 9 | **No levels, no streaks, no leaderboard** | Jiven's choice of milestones only; the research note, section 2 |

## 5. Order of work

Each step is its own commit, tests first.

1. **The score, as pure rules** (`src/core/companion/score.ts`): points, secured, came back, fading, the daily series, the steady-pace line, attribution by module.
2. **Milestones, as pure rules**, and the "needs attention" rule.
3. **The service and API:** `GET /progress` returns the new shape; study blocks gain `module`; moving a block is checked against the timetable.
4. **The Progress page:** chart, where it came from, needs attention, milestones, ideas to revisit.
5. **The Week page:** the grid, drag and drop, the module on a block.
6. **Docs and release:** `DECISIONS.md` (V-34 onward), `API.md`, `VERSIONS.md`, a release note with a test checklist; PR, CI, tag.

**How it will be checked:** table tests for every rule; the score replayed on Jiven's real history; the page and the drag looked at in a built app.
